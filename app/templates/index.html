Snack Stash Storeroom

Features

Item Tracking

Snack Stash Storeroom is first and foremost a tracking app.
Use this application to track expiration dates, item quantities, and your recipes!

Recipe Generation

Users can generate recipes by providing an image in the "Recipes" section of their pantry list.
These recipes are simple, and meant to give users an idea on how they want to create a new recipe.
Suggestions can also be given to develop a recipe based on ingredients on a user's pantry.
