Snack Stash Storeroom

Features

Snack Stash Storeroom is a versatile tool in tracking your pantry items. Users are capable of adding items to their pantry tracker, editing and deleting those items, and categorize according to their ingredient label.



Users can generate recipes by providing an image in the "Recipes" section of their pantry list. These recipes are simple, and meant to give users an idea on how they want to create a new recipe. Suggestions can also be given to develop a recipe based on ingredients on a user's pantry.
